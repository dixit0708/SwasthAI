Your Numbers

Enter your most recent measurements — check a recent lab report or physical exam if you're not sure of a number. This model was trained on adult women — if that doesn't describe you, treat the result with extra caution.

Enter 0 if this doesn't apply to you.

mg/dL

Use the reading from an oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) if you have one — this is a blood sugar test taken 2 hours after drinking a sugary drink at a lab. Check your lab report for this number.

mmHg

Blood pressure is usually written as two numbers, like 120/80 — enter the second, lower number (called diastolic pressure).

mm

This is usually measured by gently pinching the skin (called a skinfold measurement) during a physical exam. Don't worry if you're not sure — a rough estimate is fine, since this matters less to your result than blood sugar or blood pressure.

This tool provides an AI-generated risk indicator, not a medical diagnosis. It is trained on the Pima Indians Diabetes Database (adult women of Pima Indian heritage) — if that doesn't describe you, treat this result with extra caution. It is a separate model from our other diabetes assessments and its score is not directly comparable to them. Not a substitute for professional medical advice.
