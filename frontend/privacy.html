Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SwasthAI deals with health information, which is sensitive by nature. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

To create an account and use SwasthAI, we collect:

  • Account details: name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Health data you provide: inputs to risk assessments (e.g. glucose, BMI, blood pressure), uploaded medical reports or images, and any health profile or family member details you add.
  • Usage data: basic technical information (device, browser, approximate location from IP) used to keep the service secure and working correctly.

How we use it

Your data is used to run the risk-assessment and report-analysis features you ask for, to personalize your dashboard and recommendations, and to operate your account (login, appointments, family profiles). We do not sell your personal or health data to third parties, and we do not use it to train models for other users without your explicit consent.

AI-generated results

Predictions, risk scores and report summaries are generated by machine learning models based on the data you provide. They are informational only, can be wrong, and are never a diagnosis. See our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored in an access-controlled database and transmitted over encrypted connections. Only the systems and personnel required to operate the service can access it.

Your choices

  • You can view and update your health profile at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and associated data by contacting us below.
  • You can stop using any AI feature without affecting the rest of your account.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@swasthai.app.
