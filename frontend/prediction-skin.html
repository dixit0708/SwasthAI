About this tool & Limitations

Reference Dataset: Trained on clinical dermatology reference photographs from the DermNet dataset across 23 dermatological category groups.

Optimal Image Conditions: Calibrated for clear, well-focused, clinical-style close-up photographs under adequate, natural lighting.

Demographic & Quality Limitations: Public dermatology benchmark datasets historically exhibit lower representation of darker skin tones (Fitzpatrick skin types IV–VI). Pattern matching may consequently be less accurate on darker skin tones, as well as on heavily compressed phone photos, motion-blurred captures, or internet images.

No “Healthy Skin” Class: The model is trained strictly on disease category groups and does not include a separate normal/healthy skin category. It identifies the most visually similar pattern among the 23 classes; non-skin images or ambiguous healthy skin may be filtered by input checks.

Decision Support Only: This tool provides educational pattern information to facilitate informed discussions with a physician or board-certified dermatologist, and must never be used as a substitute for in-person medical examination, dermatoscope evaluation, or biopsy.
