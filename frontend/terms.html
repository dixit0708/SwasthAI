What SwasthAI is

SwasthAI is an informational health tool. It uses machine learning models to estimate risk from the data you provide, summarize uploaded reports, and offer general wellness guidance. It is designed to help you have better-informed conversations with healthcare professionals, not to replace them.

Limits of AI-generated results

  • Risk scores, report summaries and assistant responses are generated by AI models and can be inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Results depend entirely on the accuracy of the data you enter or upload.
  • No result from SwasthAI should be used on its own to start, stop, or change any treatment or medication.
  • Always confirm any concerning result with a qualified doctor before acting on it.
